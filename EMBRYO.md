# Embryo Quick Cut

Embryo Quick Cut is Embryo-Zero's fork of [LosslessCut](https://github.com/mifi/lossless-cut) (v3.70.2-75-g4c7ab1cb, commit 4c7ab1cb). It does fast lossless video and audio trimming.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
